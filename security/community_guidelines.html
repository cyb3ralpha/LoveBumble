💖 Love Bumble Community Guidelines

Welcome to Love Bumble! Our community thrives on respect, kindness, and safety. Please follow these guidelines to ensure a positive experience for everyone.

1. Be Respectful

Treat all members with respect. Harassment, hate speech, bullying, or abusive behavior will not be tolerated.

2. Age Requirement

All users must be at least 18 years old. Age verification is required before accessing the platform.

3. No Inappropriate Content

Do not share sexually explicit content, nudity, or any material that violates laws or community standards.

4. Privacy & Safety

  • Do not share personal information such as your address, phone number, or financial details with strangers.
  • Do not attempt to hack or exploit other users or the platform.

5. No Spam or Scams

Do not send spam messages, advertisements, or links to scams. Reporting such behavior is encouraged.

6. Reporting Violations

If you encounter inappropriate behavior or content, use the Report User feature. All reports will be reviewed by our moderation team.

7. Community Integrity

  • Do not impersonate others or create fake profiles.
  • Be honest in your profile and interactions.

8. Enforcement

Violating these guidelines may result in warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans depending on severity.

9. Updates

These guidelines may be updated from time to time. Please check this page periodically to stay informed.

Thank you for helping us keep Love Bumble a safe and enjoyable community! 💖
